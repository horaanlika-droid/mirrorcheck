How to hand over the card
1. A Google Gift Card of the US region, denominated in USD ($), with a full unused balance. Cards of other regions and currencies are not accepted.
2. First the order is created, then an operator connects — wait for their signal. Send nothing and share the code with no one until the operator tells you to.
3. You enter the card code in the app only after the operator connects. After you send it, the operator verifies the card (usually 5–15 minutes).
4. The payout is in BTC to the Bitcoin wallet you provide. The amount is set by the current BTC/USD rate.
5. Pricelex Worldwide support never asks for your code or money outside an order. Never share the code with anyone except the operator within your order.
6. You have 1 hour from the moment the order is created. If the card turns out to be invalid, the order is declined without payout.
